Aspect Math

Change one side and the other side has to follow.

Boundary: arithmetic only Aspect Math works out aspect ratios, resized dimensions and fits from the pixel sizes you type. It does not know the sizes, ratios or cropping rules a screen, platform or printer requires. Check those in its own specs.

The ratio truth

1920 x 1080 is 16:9 (1.78 wide for each 1 tall).

The resize truth

Scaling 1920 x 1080 to 1280 wide gives 1280 x 720.

The fit truth

Fitting 1920 x 1080 inside 1000 x 1000 gives 1000 x 563 after rounding to whole pixels, with 437 pixels spare on the height.

Run your numbers
Aspect Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
